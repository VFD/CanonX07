CASINO
Original Canon X-07 version by Maxime Tanguy
MSX adaptation by Benoît Delvaux

The casino "Bowl" game. You can play a number from 1 to 9, on even (P) or odd (I) or the series of 1 to 4 (A) and 6 to 9 (B).
